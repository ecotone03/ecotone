Quick terms, privacy & safety

Before you use ECOTONE

The quiz

The identity match is playful and interpretive. It is not a scientific, medical or psychological assessment.

The wetland facts

ECOTONE uses short, easy-to-read ecological summaries to build awareness. Named wetland sites include source links so you can learn more.

Your data

You can take the quiz without an account. If you join ECOTONE Social, your profile information is stored in Supabase so other users can discover and interact with you.

Social is 18+

Only adults should join ECOTONE Social. Compatibility scores are for fun and do not guarantee safety, friendship or romantic compatibility.

Contact sharing

Your Instagram stays private until there is a mutual match and both people separately choose to share it.

Use it safely

Do not post sensitive personal information. Use Hide or Report if a profile makes you uncomfortable. Meet new people with normal online-safety precautions.
